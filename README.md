# learn-data-governance-with-phoebe

An 8-session course that turns a data practitioner (DA / DE / DS / AI) into someone who can
run as a **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** - protect the people in the data, and protect the
company from the fine.

- **Legal lens:** Singapore **PDPA** as the home law (primary), **GDPR** as the international mirror.
- **Running case:** everything is taught by governing one company, **Himalaya** - a Singapore-based
  B2B2C SaaS platform with 1,200 business clients and 8 million end consumers, growing data-first
  and governance-last. You are its first DPO.
- **Format:** eight 45-minute sessions, easy to hard, each ending with a build-along on Himalaya,
  homework, a 3-question quiz, a sources-covered list, and a cheat sheet.

## Sessions

| # | Session | Focus |
|---|---------|-------|
| 1 | Data governance and the DPO | DAMA wheel · the DPO role · personal vs sensitive data |
| 2 | The law you enforce | 11 PDPA obligations · DNC · GDPR principles, bases, rights |
| 3 | Know your data | RoPA · classification · catalog, glossary, lineage |
| 4 | Consent, purpose & marketing (DNC) | consent model · DNC register checks |
| 5 | Protection & breach response | security arrangements · breach tabletop against the clock |
| 6 | Data lifecycle | accuracy · retention · cross-border transfer · rights |
| 7 | The operating model + maturity | policies · owners/stewards/council · maturity scoring |
| 8 | Capstone: stand up the program | DPIA · accountability · first-90-days · board report |

## Honest about scope

This course teaches the working body of knowledge. Certification exams (IAPP CIPP/E, CIPM,
CIPT and the PDPC's own courses), member-only frameworks (DCAM's full model), and legal advice
stay with their official sources. The PDPA Data Portability Obligation is taught as enacted but
not yet in force.
