One company, both tracks. Every session governs Himalaya - a Singapore-based B2B2C SaaS platform with 1,200 business clients and 8 million end consumers, growing data-first and governance-last. Practitioners take the DPO's chair and build the program session by session; executives take the CEO's chair and learn what to ask, fund, and expect.
Honest about scope. This track teaches the working body of knowledge - the DAMA governance function, the PDPA obligations and DNC, the GDPR mirror, and the DCAM maturity concept. Certification exams (IAPP CIPP/E, CIPM, CIPT and the PDPC's own courses), member-only frameworks, and legal advice stay with their official sources. The PDPA Data Portability Obligation is taught as enacted but not yet in force.
